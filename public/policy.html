5. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following specific, legitimate, and lawful purposes:

  • Scholarship Application Processing: To evaluate your eligibility for various scholarship programs
  • Intelligent Evaluation: To use fuzzy logic and prescriptive analytics algorithms to assess scholarship qualifications based on academic performance, financial need, and other relevant criteria
  • Communication: To send notifications, updates, and announcements regarding your application status via email and SMS
  • Account Management: To create, maintain, and secure your user account
  • Administrative Purposes: To maintain records, generate reports, and improve scholarship program management
  • Compliance: To meet legal, regulatory, and institutional requirements
  • System Security: To detect, prevent, and respond to security incidents and unauthorized access
  • Service Improvement: To analyze system usage and improve the functionality and user experience of iScholar

Important: Your personal information will NEVER be sold, rented, or shared for commercial marketing purposes unrelated to scholarship administration and educational services.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and limit data sharing to necessary parties only. Your information may be disclosed to:

6.1 Internal Sharing

  • Authorized administrators and scholarship committee members who require access to evaluate applications
  • Academic departments and campus officials for verification purposes
  • IT personnel for system maintenance and security (with strict confidentiality agreements)

6.2 External Sharing

  • Scholarship Providers: When you apply for external scholarships, relevant information may be shared with sponsoring organizations
  • Government Agencies: When required by law or for program compliance (e.g., CHED, DepEd, DSWD for 4Ps verification)
  • Service Providers: Third-party service providers who assist with system operations (email/SMS services, cloud storage) under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Authorities: When required by court orders, legal proceedings, or law enforcement agencies

All third parties with access to your data are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality and comply with data protection standards.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Your personal information is stored securely on servers located within the Philippines or with data processors compliant with Philippine data protection standards.

7.1 Retention Periods

  • Active Applications: Retained for the duration of the scholarship program plus two (2) academic years
  • Approved/Awarded Scholarships: Retained for seven (7) years from the date of graduation or program completion, in accordance with institutional record-keeping requirements
  • Denied Applications: Retained for three (3) years from the date of denial for statistical and compliance purposes
  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active and for two (2) years after account closure
  • System Logs: Retained for one (1) year for security and audit purposes

After retention periods expire, personal data will be securely deleted or anonymized unless continued retention is required by law or for legitimate archival purposes.

8. Data Security Measures

We implement robust organizational, physical, and technical security measures to protect your personal information:

8.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: Passwords are encrypted using industry-standard Argon2id hashing algorithm
  • Secure Transmission: Data transmission is protected using SSL/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Role-based access restrictions ensure only authorized personnel can view sensitive information
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication options for enhanced account security
  • Audit Trails: System activity logs track all access and modifications to personal data

8.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Regular security awareness training for staff
  • Confidentiality agreements for all personnel with data access
  • Data protection impact assessments
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Incident response and breach notification procedures

8.3 Physical Safeguards

  • Secure server facilities with restricted access
  • Backup systems and disaster recovery plans
  • Secure disposal procedures for physical and electronic records
